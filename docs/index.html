Showing 12 finds

01
Geology · Free

Look into “The Rock”

A public overlook peers into the world’s largest open-face granite quarry—roughly 66 football fields of white Mount Airy granite.

Free20–40 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Go on an operating day, stay in the public viewing area, and listen for the warning siren before a quarry blast.

151 Granite Quarry Trail
Directions ↗Quarry history
02
Art · Self-guided

Hunt the walls downtown

A free 12-stop art walk leads beyond the famous photo ops to Melva Houston, Donna Fargo, ghost signs, contemporary murals, and brick sculpture.

Free45–75 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Do not miss the Whittling Wall on West Oak Street—eight local figures are sculpted directly from brick.

Start near 200 N. Main Street
Directions ↗Art walk details
03
Music · Thursday night

Join the old-time jam

The Earle Theatre is not just a museum. On Thursday nights, musicians and listeners gather for a free, come-as-you-are old-time and bluegrass session.

FreeThu · 7 p.m.
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Bring an instrument if you play—or simply take a seat. The session starts at 7 p.m. and visitors are welcome.

142 N. Main Street
Directions ↗Jam session details
04
Music · Saturday morning

Sit inside a live radio show

WPAQ’s Merry-Go-Round broadcasts live from the Earle every Saturday. It has aired since 1948 and is second in longevity only to the Grand Ole Opry.

$8Sat · 11–1:30
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

The show runs 11 a.m.–1:30 p.m. Admission is $8, or included with an Andy Griffith Museum ticket bought that day.

142 N. Main Street
Directions ↗Broadcast details
05
Outdoors · Bring your gear

Paddle through town

The Ararat River Greenway hides several canoe, kayak, and tube launches along a restored, trout-stocked river corridor.

Free access1–3 hours
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Riverside Park is an easy place to begin. Check water conditions, wear a PFD, and arrange your take-out before launching.

350 Riverside Drive
Directions ↗Trail and river details
06
Roadside art · Seasonal

Follow the ice-cream path

Behind a converted camper serving ice cream, a path winds into the woods through hand-built metal sculptures and even a small forest chapel.

Treats $30–60 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

The sculpture garden is the real surprise. Scoops is seasonal, so confirm opening days before making the drive west of town.

5091 W. Pine Street
Directions ↗Season and location
07
Food · Local dessert

Taste a local cobbler

Warm fruit, a baked crust, and a scoop-worthy finish make cobbler an easy sweet stop while exploring Mount Airy.

$20–40 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Anchored Bakery is a convenient Mount Airy trail stop. Ask what fruit is in the oven; small batches can sell out.

139 Moore Avenue
Directions ↗Bakery details
08
Food · Depression-era local

Order ground steak “all the way”

This loose, seasoned beef sandwich was created locally to stretch meat during the Great Depression—and rarely travels beyond Surry County.

$30–60 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Try the Dairy Center’s version with tomato, slaw, and mayo. The family-run stop has served Mount Airy since 1954.

407 W. Lebanon Street
Directions ↗Ground Steak Trail
09
History · After dark

Walk Mayberry by lantern

After the storefronts quiet down, a 90-minute tour trades TV nostalgia for true local stories and 13 of Mount Airy’s “less mortal” residents.

$2090 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

The 2026 walking tours depart Fridays and Saturdays at 8 p.m., May through October. Advance tickets are recommended.

301 N. Main Street
Directions ↗2026 tour schedule
10
History · Two-stop story

Follow Chang & Eng’s real story

A family-informed museum tells the complicated lives of Chang and Eng Bunker. Their shared grave is a short drive away at White Plains Baptist Church.

Grave free · museum $1–2 hours
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Pair the museum with the grave for the full story. The cemetery is an active, sacred place—visit quietly and respectfully.

215 Rockford Street · 614 Old US 601
Directions ↗Museum and history
11
Historic home · Free

Step into Gertrude’s 1903 home

This remarkably intact Main Street house remains furnished as the Smith family lived in it, right down to Gertrude’s art and interior details.

Free45–60 min
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Admission is free. It is normally open April–December on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday from 11 a.m.–4 p.m.

708 N. Main Street
Directions ↗Current visitor details
12
Local scene · Weekends

Slip behind Main to Market Street

One block off the tourist strip, this arts district turns into a pedestrian hangout with makers, breweries, food, and rotating live music.

Free to browse1–3 hours
KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

From May through October, weekends are the sweet spot. Check the downtown calendar for Melva’s Alley music and maker events.

Your Saturday, solved

One day. No filler.

This route strings together seven of the best finds without racing you around town. It works best on a Saturday during ghost-tour season.

  1. 8:30AM

    Breakfast like a local

    Ground steak and egg—or the classic sandwich—at the Dairy Center.

  2. 9:45AM

    See The Rock

    Drive up to the public quarry overlook before downtown fills in.

  3. 11:00AM

    Go live at the Earle

    Take a seat for WPAQ’s Saturday Merry-Go-Round broadcast.

  4. 1:45PM

    Find the cobbler

    Walk to Anchored Bakery and ask which flavor came out today.

  5. 2:30PM

    Collect the walls

    Follow the public art walk to the Whittling Wall and Market Street.

  6. 4:15PM

    Cool off by the Ararat

    Walk, fish, or paddle the river corridor from Riverside Park.

  7. 8:00PM

    Meet the less mortal

    Finish with the seasonal lantern-lit downtown ghost tour.

Before you roll in

Three things locals already know

01

Thursday and Saturday win.

Thursday has the free jam. Saturday has live radio, the Market Street scene, and seasonal ghost tours.

02

“Open” can be seasonal.

Scoops, the historic house, and night tours change with the season. Use the detail links before driving.

03

Pack for the second half.

Comfortable shoes, water, bug spray, and a rain layer turn the art walk and river stop into an actual good time.
